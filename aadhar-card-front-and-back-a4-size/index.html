What is the Aadhar Card Print Size Width and Height?

When printed as a smart card (PVC), the exact dimensions of an Aadhar card follow the ISO CR80 standard:

  • Aadhar card front and back a4 size in cm: 8.5 cm (width) × 5.5 cm (height).
  • Aadhar card front and back a4 size in inches: 3.375 inches × 2.125 inches.
  • Aadhar card front and back a4 size in pixels: At a standard 300 DPI (Dots Per Inch) print resolution, the card should be roughly 1004 pixels wide by 650 pixels high.

Our tool uses these exact measurements. When the final A4 page is printed at 100% scale (no margins), the cards will print exactly at physical PVC card size.
