What is an ENC File and Why Can’t Windows or Mac Open It?

If you have recently transferred a backup from your Android phone or downloaded a WhatsApp archive to your computer via USB, you may notice that your images end with .enc instead of a standard .jpg or .png.

An ENC file is a secure, encrypted container. It is not an image format. Applications like WhatsApp, Samsung Secure Folder, or even PC-based encryption tools (like CopySafe) scramble the binary data of standard files to protect them from unauthorized access.

When you double-click an ENC file on Windows 10/11 or macOS, the default "Photos" or "Preview" apps will fail to load the image. These programs look for specific JPEG structural headers to understand how to paint the pixels on your screen. Because the ENC file is encrypted, those headers look like random mathematical garbage. The file must be properly decrypted before a PC image viewer can read it.

Why Do Files Turn Into ENC? (Root Cause & Prevention)

Understanding where these files came from can help you avoid dealing with them in the future.

  • Transferring Encrypted Mobile Backups: If you copy the msgstore or Media folders from your Android to your PC via USB cable, you are copying raw, encrypted data. Usually, the phone decrypts this data on the fly when you open the gallery app.
  • S-63 Maritime Navigation Charts: Professionals in the maritime industry use .enc files to securely distribute digital maps. These require specific cryptographic keys to convert into viewable PDFs or images.
  • Desktop Encryption Software: Tools like AxCrypt or CopySafe will intentionally append .enc to files on your hard drive to protect them from hackers or snooping family members.

Why does my converted JPG show a 0KB file size?

Do I need a decryption key to convert ENC files on PC?

Why are the files from my Android showing up as ENC on my PC?
