Percentage Formula: How to Calculate Percentage of Marks

The percentage formula is one of the most fundamental mathematical calculations every student needs. Whether you just received your 10th board results, 12th class marksheet, or university semester grades, calculating your overall percentage is the first thing you do. The universal formula is:

Percentage (%) = (Total Marks Obtained ÷ Total Maximum Marks) × 100

How to Calculate Percentage of Marks of 5 Subjects

If you have 5 subjects (common in ICSE or some state boards), the calculation is straightforward. Suppose your marks are:

  • English: 85/100
  • Maths: 92/100
  • Science: 78/100
  • Social Studies: 88/100
  • Hindi: 75/100

Total Obtained: 85 + 92 + 78 + 88 + 75 = 418
Total Maximum: 5 × 100 = 500
Percentage: (418 / 500) × 100 = 83.6%

How to Calculate Percentage of Marks of 6 Subjects

For CBSE and most state boards, students have 6 subjects in their 10th and 12th examinations. The process is identical — you simply add marks for all 6 subjects. For example, if you scored 480 out of 600, your percentage is (480/600) × 100 = 80%.

Our 10th mark percentage calculator above automates this. Just type in your 6 subject marks and hit Calculate.

How to Calculate Percentage of Marks of 7 Subjects

Some boards include an additional subject (like a second language or vocational subject), bringing the total to 7. The formula remains the same — simply add all 7 obtained marks, divide by the total maximum (e.g., 700), and multiply by 100.

How to Calculate Percentage of Marks of 12th Class

Calculating the percentage of marks of 12th class is critical because it directly impacts college admissions, scholarship eligibility, and competitive exam cutoffs. Most 12th boards have 5 main subjects. Some students calculate "best of 4" or "best of 5" depending on the university's requirements.

Our tool lets you add as many subjects as needed and instantly get the result.

How to Calculate Percentage of 6 Semesters

For undergraduate students (B.Tech, BCA, B.Com, BA, etc.), the overall percentage is typically the average of all semester percentages or an aggregate calculated from total marks across all semesters.

  • Method 1 (Average): Add all semester percentages and divide by the number of semesters.
  • Method 2 (Aggregate): Add all marks obtained across all semesters and divide by total maximum marks across all semesters.

How to Calculate Percentage of Marks in Grade System

Many modern boards (CBSE, ICSE) and universities use a grade-point system (CGPA) instead of raw percentage. To convert:

CBSE Formula: Percentage = CGPA × 9.5
Example: If your CGPA is 8.6, your percentage = 8.6 × 9.5 = 81.7%

Is 75% a good percentage?

Yes. 75% is generally considered a First Division in most Indian boards. 60%+ is First Division, 45–59% is Second Division, and 33–44% is Third Division (Pass).
