What About HEVC Video?

A common point of confusion is the difference between HEIC and HEVC. Users often ask how to convert heic to hevc. This is a misunderstanding of formats.

  • HEIC (High-Efficiency Image Container) is for photos.
  • HEVC (High-Efficiency Video Coding / H.265) is for videos.

You cannot convert an image into a video format directly. If you have an iPhone Live Photo, it contains both a HEIC photo and an HEVC (MOV) video. Our tool extracts and converts the HEIC photo portion into a standard JPG. If you need to convert an HEVC video file, you will need a dedicated video transcoder like the open-source software Handbrake.
