What is a PDF Watermark and Why Do You Need It?

A watermark is a recognizable image or text overlaid onto a document, typically with a degree of transparency so that the original content remains visible. In the context of digital PDF files, a watermark serves several vital purposes:

  • Copyright Protection: Visual PDF watermarks prevent unauthorized use or distribution of your intellectual property.
  • Document Status Indication: Large text watermarks like "CONFIDENTIAL," "DRAFT," or "DO NOT COPY" instantly communicate the nature and status of the document.
  • Branding: Businesses often add a subtle logo watermark to the background of their invoices, reports, and proposals.
