Troubleshooting Common OCR Errors

"The extracted text is garbled or nonsense."

The image resolution is likely too low, or the text is too blurry. OCR needs sharp, high-contrast edges. Try sharpening the image or increasing its contrast before uploading.

"Missing spaces or line breaks."

The text in the image may be formatted in complex columns (like a newspaper). Crop the image into smaller, single-column sections before processing.

"No Text Found" error.

The text color may be too similar to the background. Ensure high contrast. Convert the image to pure black and white before uploading if necessary.
