Why Can't I Just Use Pixels? Understanding DPI

When dealing with digital screens, we use "pixels." But when dealing with printed ID cards or formal application forms, authorities use physical measurements like cm or mm. The bridge between these two worlds is called DPI (Dots Per Inch) or PPI (Pixels Per Inch).

  • If a portal requires a 3.5 cm x 4.5 cm photo, they are assuming a high-quality print resolution of 300 DPI.
  • At 300 DPI, 3.5 cm x 4.5 cm mathematically translates to exactly 413 pixels by 531 pixels.

If you don't know the math, you don't need to worry! Our resize image in cm online change image size free in pixels converter automatically handles all the DPI conversions in the background. Simply type in the CM or MM values, and we will output a perfectly compliant resize image in cm jpg file.
